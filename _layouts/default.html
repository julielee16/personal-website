About 소개

Juhoon (Julie) Lee · she/her

I study what gets lost when machines carry our words across languages and how to carry the rest.

I am a Ph.D. student at the KAIST School of Computing, working with Prof. Joseph Seering in the Collaborative Social Technologies Lab (CSTL🏰).

My research tackles the social and cultural flattening that happens in cross-lingual, AI-mediated communication. Combining HCI methodologies with NLP, I work on redefining translation as a situated, socially embedded process beyond a semantic exchange. I explore how the context, the relationship between speakers, and the culture drive how the message and intent are communicated.

I aim to build foundational frameworks where translation technologies become transparent and contextually rich.
